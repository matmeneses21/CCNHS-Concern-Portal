About This Website

A safe and confidential reporting platform developed for the students of Calasiao Comprehensive National High School.

Why This Portal Exists

CCNHS Concern Mo, Action Namin! was created to provide every learner with a secure and respectful way to report concerns affecting their well-being, safety, and learning experience.

Reports submitted through this portal will be reviewed by the authorized SSLG Aid and Welfare Committee and designated school personnel.
